**Outcome:** Worked on a team to design and prototype Digit, a wearable mechanical thumb capable of actuation and grasping. The project applied the engineering design process, 3D modelling, and machine shop skills. We also assessed the toy’s mechanical viability for potential further development by a company.

<br clear="right">

**Experience:** I designed several core mechanical components, including the hand mount and an elastic extension mechanism that restored the thumb to its neutral position. I modelled the mount in SolidWorks and iterated the components through functional testing.

In my initial design, I proposed a component that slid onto the user’s thumb and attached to a string to move Digit. Testing showed that this defeated Digit’s role as an additional finger because it relied on the user’s thumb for actuation.

To address this, I transitioned to wrist-based actuation, freeing all five fingers while enabling controlled movement of Digit. We used this design for the final iteration.

**Learning:** I learned how to create independent mechanical motion paths connected to specific hand movements, preventing wrist rotation from unintentionally triggering extension or retraction. I also learned to use tension and varying string lengths to actuate Digit.
